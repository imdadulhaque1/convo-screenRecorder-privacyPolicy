1. Information we collect

None. Convo Recorder does not collect personal information, device identifiers, advertising IDs, location, contacts, usage statistics or crash reports. There is no developer-run server and the app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it has no way to transmit data over the internet.

Everything the app creates or reads stays on your phone, under your control.

2. What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app creates the following items. They are stored locally and are never uploaded:

  • Your videos. Saved recordings go to Movies/ConvoRecorder/Recordings, edited videos to Movies/ConvoRecorder/Exports, or to a folder you pick yourself. If the save folder does not exist yet, the app creates it, either when you tap "Create folder" in Settings or automatically the first time you save a video. The app looks inside that folder only to show you whether it exists and how many videos it holds.
  • An unsaved recording. After you stop recording, the video is kept in the app's private storage until you press Save or Cancel. Cancel deletes it. It is not visible to other apps or in your gallery until you save it.
  • Custom thumbnails and markers. A cover picture you choose and the markers you add while recording are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Settings. Your choices (sound, camera, folder, theme and similar) are stored in the app's private preferences.
  • A local error log. If the app ever crashes, a technical error description is written to a file inside the app's private storage so the app can tell you about it on the next start. It is not sent anywhere.

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, so these items are not copied to your Google account by the system either.

4. Sharing and files you choose

  • Sharing a video. Only when you tap Share. The video is handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Messenger or Telegram) through Android. That app's own privacy policy applies from that point. Convo Recorder does not see or keep a copy of what you send.
  • Apps shown in the share sheet. To show the apps that can receive a video, the app asks Android which installed apps accept videos. This list is used on your device to draw the screen and is never stored or sent.
  • Opening your folder. The "Open" button hands your save folder to a Files app on your phone. To find one, the app asks Android whether common Files apps are installed. Nothing is stored or sent.
  • Folder, music and pictures. If you pick a save folder, a song for background music, or a picture for a thumbnail, the app can only use the item you picked and only on your device.
  • Hiding private details. The editor can blur, pixelate or black out parts of a video. This happens on your device when you save the edit.

5. Third parties

Convo Recorder contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDK that collects data. It uses standard open-source Android libraries (such as Jetpack Compose, AndroidX Media3 and CameraX) that run entirely on your device. No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party.

6. Security

Because your data never leaves your phone, its safety depends on your phone's own protection. Unsaved recordings, settings and thumbnails are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Videos you save go to your normal media folders, just like photos and videos from your camera, so other apps you have given media access to can see them. Keep your device locked and updated.

Android may show some secure screens (banking apps, for example) as black in a recording. That is Android protecting those apps, not a fault of Convo Recorder.

7. Retention and deletion

  • We keep nothing, because we hold no data about you.
  • Delete a recording any time from the app's Library, or from your phone's file manager or gallery.
  • Pressing Cancel while reviewing a new recording deletes it immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its settings, thumbnails, markers and logs. Videos you saved in your Movies folder (or your chosen folder) are your files and stay on your phone until you delete them.

8. Children

Convo Recorder is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, no data from children is collected either.

9. Recording other people

A screen recording can capture messages, names, passwords, notifications and the voices or faces of other people. You are responsible for using the app lawfully: please get consent where the law or common courtesy requires it, and be careful about what is on screen before you record or share.
